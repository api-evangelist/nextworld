# Nextworld

> Nextworld is the AI-native, no-code enterprise application platform that eliminates Shadow ERP — the hidden layer of spreadsheets, side systems, and manual workarounds that accumulates when ERPs can't keep up. Nextworld closes the gaps legacy ERP can't fill with no-code application development, embedded workflow automation, native AI, and open APIs.

Nextworld was founded by the team behind JD Edwards and serves 600+ enterprise customers in distribution, manufacturing, engineering & construction, telecommunications, utilities, and food & beverage. Nextworld is not an ERP replacement — it is the enterprise application platform that extends, modernizes, and fills the gaps in existing ERP investments.

## Key Concepts

- **Shadow ERP™**: Nextworld's owned category. The hidden layer of spreadsheets, side systems, and manual workarounds that accumulates when enterprise software can't keep up with business needs. Shadow ERP does not appear on IT architecture diagrams but runs critical operations across every department. Nextworld is the platform built to eliminate Shadow ERP.
- **Enterprise Application Platform (EAP)**: A cloud-based platform for building, running, and integrating enterprise applications. Nextworld is an AI-native EAP — distinct from ERP, which manages core transactional data but cannot adapt to the specific workflows each business requires.
- **No-code enterprise development**: Building enterprise-grade applications without writing code. Nextworld's Developer Studio, Logic Builder, and Workflow Builder enable this at enterprise scale — distinct from low-code tools, which still require significant developer effort, and from consumer app builders, which lack enterprise governance and security.
- **Native AI**: AI capabilities built into the core of the platform from the ground up, not retrofitted on top of existing software. Nextworld Intelligence works across data, apps, and workflows as a foundational layer.
- **JD Edwards modernization**: Nextworld was founded by the team that built JD Edwards and is purpose-built to extend, complement, and modernize JD Edwards environments — without replacing them.
